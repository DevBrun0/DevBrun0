### 👨‍💻 About me

Software engineer and **CTO**, focused on turning complex business problems into products that ship. These days I lead the engineering of a **credit intelligence platform** for debt recovery, a mix of fintech and legaltech: data pipelines, document processing, risk analysis and the product around it.

- 🏗️ **Building:** full-stack SaaS with Next.js, TypeScript, Firebase/Postgres and Python workers
- ☁️ **Running:** infrastructure on AWS and GCP, with cost and performance in mind
- 🤖 **Exploring:** LLMs applied to real workflows (document extraction, classification, agents)
- 🧭 **Leading:** architecture decisions, code review and turning roadmaps into delivery
- 🇧🇷 Based in Brazil · Portuguese and English
